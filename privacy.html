Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AI Pulse Daily is an automated content-production tool operated by an individual creator. It generates short news-explainer videos and publishes them to social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) on behalf of its own operator.

What this app does

This app connects only to social media accounts owned and controlled by its operator, using each platform's official API and the permissions the operator explicitly grants. It uses those permissions solely to upload and manage video content on the operator's own Page/channel/account.

What data we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from viewers of the published content. We do not use cookies or tracking on this page. The only data the app handles is: (1) API access tokens needed to publish content to the operator's own connected accounts, and (2) publicly available news headlines used as source material for video scripts.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. Access tokens are used only to communicate directly with the relevant platform's own API (Meta, TikTok, Google/YouTube) and are not shared elsewhere.

Data retention

Access tokens are retained only for as long as needed to operate the app and are revocable at any time by the operator through the relevant platform's own account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app operator through the relevant platform's developer contact channels.
