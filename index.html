AI Pulse Daily Automation

Short, original daily video explainers on AI and tech news.

AI Pulse Daily Automation is a self-operated content pipeline that turns the day's most notable AI and technology news stories into short, plain-language video explainers. Every video is generated fresh from an original script — never a repost or reupload of someone else's footage — and published on a fixed daily schedule.

How it works

1Trending AI and tech stories are sourced from a curated set of technology news feeds and filtered for relevance.
2An original script and title are written for each story — no two videos share the same script.
3The script is narrated with synthesized voiceover and paired with topical stock visuals and captions.
4The finished video is published to the operator's own connected accounts on each platform below.

Where our videos appear

YouTube (long-form + Shorts) Facebook Reels TikTok

Videos post on a fixed cadence: two short-form videos per day, and a long-form video Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday.

About the operator

AI Pulse Daily Automation is built and run by a single independent creator as a personal content-automation project. It connects only to accounts the operator owns and controls, using each platform's official API within the permissions the operator explicitly grants.
