Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

AI Pulse Daily is an automated content-production tool operated by an individual creator, used to generate and publish short news-explainer videos to social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook, TikTok) on the operator's own accounts.

Scope

This app is a single-operator tool. It is not offered as a public service for third parties to sign up for or use, and it does not manage accounts other than those explicitly connected by its operator.

Use of platform APIs

This app accesses each platform's official API only within the scope of permissions the operator grants, and only to publish and manage content on the operator's own connected Page/channel/account.

Content

Video content is produced from publicly available news sources, with an original script and analysis written for each video rather than a direct reproduction of source material.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time as the app's functionality changes.
